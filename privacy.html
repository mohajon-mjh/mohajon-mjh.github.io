Last updated: August 2026

Mohajon MJH is an international online marketplace operating worldwide. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email, phone number)

  • Order history and preferences
  • Payment information (processed by secure third parties)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To communicate with you about orders and support
  • To improve our marketplace and user experience

3. Authentication & Google Sign-In

When you choose "Continue with Google", you are redirected to Google's official OAuth login page. We never see or store your Google password. Google shares only your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) with us after your consent.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your login session and remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Delivery partners (to fulfill orders)
  • Payment processors (to process transactions securely)
  • Legal authorities (when required by law)

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and secure Firebase authentication to protect your data.

8. International Operations

As an international marketplace, your data may be processed in multiple countries, including the USA (via Firebase/Google Cloud), always under strict data protection standards.
